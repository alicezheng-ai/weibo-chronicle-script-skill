# Example (not yet included)

`reference/piece.jsx` currently contains real personal content from the
archive it was built against. Before publishing, replace it with either your
own material or a public-domain source, and add the corresponding
`chronicle.md` and `beats.json` here so readers can see the handoff format
without supplying data of their own.

A public-domain diary with a long date range works well, and re-running the
pipeline on unfamiliar material is also the honest test of whether the metaphor
relay generalizes.
